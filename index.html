Khepri is a privately operated, local-first artificial intelligence assistant designed to support everyday activities through natural voice interaction, calendar assistance, and authorized software integrations.

About Khepri

Khepri is an independently developed personal AI assistant operated on privately controlled computing hardware. It is intended for individual use and is not offered as a public AI service.

Khepri combines local language processing with permission-controlled tools to help its operator retrieve information and manage personal activities.

Google Calendar Integration

Khepri uses the Google Calendar API to access calendar information with the Google account holder's explicit authorization.

Its current integration requests read-only calendar permissions. These permissions allow Khepri to identify accessible calendars and retrieve event information to answer scheduling questions and help evaluate availability.

Khepri does not require public access to the assistant or its local computer for this integration. Calendar authorization is managed separately through Google's OAuth consent process.

Privacy and User Control

Khepri is designed around local operation, explicit authorization, and controlled access to external services.

Details about Google account permissions, data use, storage, and authorization controls are provided in the Privacy Policy.
